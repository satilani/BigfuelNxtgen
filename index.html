About Bigfuel Nxtgen

Engineered for Scale & Reliability

Bigfuel Nxtgen India Private Limited operates a dedicated 30 KLPD modular batch process refining facility at Vill.- Bharawan, Sandila, Hardoi, Uttar Pradesh, India.

Designed for multi-feedstock versatility, our plant efficiently converts UCO, Non-Edible Oils and Fats into high-purity Biodiesel (B100) for Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs) and bulk industrial consumers, while recovering valuable oleochemical co-products.

Core Process Engineering

Refinery Architecture

Our 30 KLPD modular batch process refining architecture integrates five core purification and conversion technologies for batch-to-batch consistency:

30 KLPD Modular Batch Process Refining: Automated temperature, agitation, and dosing controls delivering high ester yield and complete batch traceability.
Multi-Feedstock Flexibility: Dual-stage esterification and transesterification engineered to process UCO, Non-Edible Oils and Fats across varying FFA profiles.
Dry-Wash Ion-Exchange Resin Purification: Waterless selective resin columns adsorbing residual soaps, catalysts, and free glycerin without effluent generation.
Vacuum Dehydration: Sub-atmospheric moisture stripping and closed-loop methanol recovery maintaining water levels well below 500 mg/kg.
Centrifuge Filtration: High-speed centrifugal separation and multi-stage polishing filters ensuring optical clarity and fuel-injector safety.

End-to-End Batch Execution

8-Step 30 KLPD Modular Batch Process

Zero-Effluent Closed-Loop Refining
01

60T Weighment & Intake

Electronic 60-tonne weighbridge verification and FFA sampling of incoming UCO, non-edible oils, and fats into the 450 KL tank farm.

02

Degumming & Pre-Treatment

Particulate filtration, moisture removal, and thermal conditioning prior to catalytic batch dosing.

03

Acid Esterification

Targeted conversion of Free Fatty Acids (FFA) into methyl esters to prevent soap formation.

04

Batch Transesterification

PLC-controlled modular batch reaction yielding FAME biodiesel and gravity-separated crude glycerol.

05

Methanol Recovery

Distillation rectification towers recovering excess methanol for closed-loop reuse in subsequent batches.

06

Vacuum Dehydration

Sub-atmospheric flash drying reducing moisture content well below the 500 mg/kg BIS limit.

07

Dry-Wash Resin & Centrifuge

Waterless ion-exchange resin beds followed by high-speed centrifuge filtration for crystal-clear B100.

08

Lab Certification & Dispatch

Pre-dispatch BIS IS 15607:2022 batch approval in our Wet Chemistry Lab and calibrated OMC tanker loading.
